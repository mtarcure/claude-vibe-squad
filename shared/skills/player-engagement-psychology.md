# Player Engagement Psychology

Design motivation and retention honestly: intrinsic drivers, flow, and reward schedules that respect the player rather than exploit them.

## Steps
1. Identify the player's intrinsic motivators (autonomy, competence, relatedness) the design should serve.
2. Design for flow: match challenge to skill, give clear goals and immediate feedback, and avoid boredom/anxiety edges.
3. Choose reward structure and cadence deliberately; document any variable-reward or retention mechanic and its intent.
4. Flag any dark pattern (manipulative FOMO, predatory monetization, addictive dark loops) for review — do not ship it silently.
5. Define the engagement signals to measure and the ethical line the design will not cross.

## Acceptance
- Engagement is grounded in named intrinsic motivators and flow, not just extrinsic hooks.
- Reward/retention mechanics are documented with intent.
- Any manipulative/dark pattern is flagged for review, never shipped silently.
