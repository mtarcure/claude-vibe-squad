# Audio Layering Techniques

Build a rich sound from layered elements — sub, body, transient, texture — that combine without masking or phase problems.

## Steps
1. Assign each layer a job and a frequency range (low/sub, body/mid, transient/attack, air/texture) so layers complement rather than compete.
2. Carve overlapping frequencies with EQ so no two layers fight for the same band (frequency slotting).
3. Align transients and check phase/polarity between layers; correct cancellation before it reaches the mix.
4. Balance levels and stereo placement so the composite reads as one sound with intended weight and width.
5. Reference the composite mono and at low volume; hand the final mix decision to `sound-design-principles`/`audio-production-basics`.

## Acceptance
- Each layer has a defined role and frequency band; overlaps are EQ-slotted.
- Phase/polarity between layers is checked; no unintended cancellation.
- The composite holds up in mono and at low listening level.
