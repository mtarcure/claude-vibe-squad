# Sonic Branding

Design a brand's audio identity — logo/mnemonic, motifs, and usage rules — that is recognizable, consistent, and ownable.

## Steps
1. Translate brand attributes into sonic qualities (tempo, timbre, instrumentation, mood) with a rationale, not a vibe.
2. Design the audio logo/mnemonic (a short, memorable signature) and the motifs that extend it across contexts.
3. Define a system: variations for different lengths/media (app, ad, hold music, UI), all traceably from the core.
4. Write usage rules (where, how long, what not to do) so the identity stays consistent across producers.
5. Ensure originality and clearance — no derivative of a protected mnemonic; flag rights concerns for review.

## Acceptance
- Sonic qualities map to brand attributes with stated rationale.
- A core mnemonic plus a documented variation system covers the required contexts.
- Usage rules exist; originality/clearance is verified, not assumed.
