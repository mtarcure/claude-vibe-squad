# Narrative Pacing

Control the rhythm of a linear piece — video, story, or presentation — so tension, rest, and payoff land where intended.

## Steps
1. Map the emotional/energy arc the audience should experience across the runtime.
2. Structure beats and their durations to build and release tension; place rests so payoffs hit harder.
3. Control information reveal: what the audience knows and when, to sustain curiosity without confusion.
4. Cut or tighten anything that stalls the arc; verify transitions carry momentum between beats.
5. Check pacing against the target length and medium; distinguish arc/rhythm from `narrative-structure` (which sets the arc itself).

## Acceptance
- An explicit energy/tension arc maps to beat durations across the runtime.
- Information reveal sustains curiosity; nothing stalls the arc unaddressed.
- Pacing fits the target length/medium and complements (not duplicates) narrative structure.
