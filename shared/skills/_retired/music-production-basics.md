# Music Production Basics

Take a musical idea to a finished track — arrangement, sound selection, mix, and structure — that serves the brief.

## Steps
1. Define the brief: genre, tempo, mood, reference tracks, and where the music will be used (length/format).
2. Arrange for structure and energy: sections, transitions, and the arc the listener should feel over time.
3. Select and process sounds so parts occupy distinct frequency and stereo space.
4. Mix by balance → EQ/dynamics → space; leave headroom for mastering (hand off to `audio-production-basics`).
5. Verify against the references and the delivery format; clear any sampled/recognizable material for rights before use.

## Acceptance
- Arrangement has a deliberate structure and energy arc tied to the brief.
- Parts occupy distinct frequency/stereo space; the mix has headroom.
- Samples/recognizable material are rights-cleared, not assumed free.
