# Video Production Principles

Plan and direct video so shots serve the story — shot language, coverage, continuity, and lighting — before post ever starts.

## Steps
1. Derive the shot list from the script/brief: what each shot must show and why it earns screen time.
2. Choose shot size, angle, and movement as language (establish, isolate, reveal), not decoration.
3. Ensure coverage and continuity (screen direction, eyeline, 180° rule) so the edit can actually cut together.
4. Plan lighting and framing to direct attention and set mood consistently across setups.
5. Hand a coherent set of shots and notes to post (`video-post-production`); flag any shot that can't be captured as a requirement gap.

## Acceptance
- Every planned shot has a stated narrative purpose.
- Coverage and continuity rules are respected so the footage cuts together.
- Lighting/framing are intentional; uncapturable shots are flagged, not silently dropped.
