# Audio Production Basics

The shared production chain under any audio work — capture, edit, mix, master — with honest levels and no clipping.

## Steps
1. Set up the signal chain and gain staging so nothing clips and headroom is preserved end to end.
2. Edit for cleanliness: trim, fades, noise/click removal, and timing without introducing artifacts.
3. Mix by balance first (levels, pan), then corrective/creative EQ and dynamics, then space (reverb/delay) — in that order.
4. Master to the target loudness standard (e.g. streaming/broadcast LUFS) with true-peak limiting; state the target, don't guess.
5. Verify on multiple playback systems and in mono; export to the required format/spec.

## Acceptance
- Gain staging preserves headroom; no clipping or true-peak overs.
- The mix follows balance → EQ/dynamics → space, not random tweaks.
- Master hits a stated loudness target and holds up across playback systems and mono.
