# Copy Refinement

Tighten existing copy for clarity, voice, and correctness without changing its meaning or inventing claims.

## Steps
1. Read for intent: what the copy must do and who it addresses; preserve that job through every edit.
2. Cut redundancy and hedging; make each sentence earn its place and each claim verifiable.
3. Enforce voice/tone and terminology consistency (cross-check `voice-consistency-audit` / `terminology-memory`).
4. Fix grammar, mechanics, and structure; improve flow and scannability (headings, front-loading).
5. Verify no factual claim was introduced or distorted; flag any claim that needs a source rather than asserting it.

## Acceptance
- Meaning is preserved; the copy is tighter and clearer, not just different.
- Voice and terminology are consistent with the established standard.
- No new/unsupported claims introduced; claims needing sources are flagged.
