# Platform Compliance

Fit a media deliverable to a platform's technical and policy requirements before publish — specs, safe areas, and rules.

## Steps
1. Pull the target platform's current spec: resolution, aspect ratio(s), duration limits, codec/bitrate, captions, loudness.
2. Lay out for the platform's safe areas and UI overlays so nothing critical is cropped or covered.
3. Check content-policy and disclosure rules (ads/sponsorship/AI-disclosure/age) that apply to the piece; flag anything uncertain for review.
4. Meet accessibility requirements (captions/audio description) the platform mandates.
5. Produce a per-platform export checklist; verify the actual file against it before publishing.

## Acceptance
- The deliverable meets the platform's current technical spec (verified, not remembered).
- Safe areas and mandated accessibility (captions/AD) are satisfied.
- Policy/disclosure requirements are checked; uncertainties are flagged, not assumed compliant.
