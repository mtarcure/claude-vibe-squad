# Video Post-Production

Run the edit-to-delivery pipeline — assembly, cut, sound, titles, grade, export — so a rough edit becomes a finished deliverable.

## Steps
1. Assemble against the script/brief; establish the story spine before polishing.
2. Refine the cut for pacing and continuity (coordinate rhythm with `narrative-pacing`); remove what doesn't earn its place.
3. Finish audio: dialogue clarity, levels, music, and SFX to a stated loudness target (hand off to `audio-production-basics`).
4. Add titles/graphics and grade (see `color-grading-basics`); keep them consistent with brand and readable.
5. Export to each platform's spec (resolution, codec, aspect, loudness); verify the render before shipping.

## Acceptance
- The cut serves the story spine and holds continuity/pacing.
- Audio hits a stated loudness target; titles/grade are consistent and readable.
- Exports match each delivery spec and are verified, not assumed correct.
