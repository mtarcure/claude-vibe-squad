# Color Grading Basics

Grade video for a consistent, intentional look — correct first, then stylize — using scopes, not just the eye.

## Steps
1. Correct before you grade: set white balance, exposure, and contrast using scopes (waveform/vectorscope), not guesswork.
2. Match shots within a scene so cuts don't jump in brightness or color.
3. Apply the creative look (mood, palette) deliberately; keep it consistent across the sequence.
4. Protect skin tones and legal broadcast/delivery ranges; avoid clipping highlights/crushing shadows unless intended.
5. Verify on a calibrated reference and at the delivery color space/format; note assumptions when calibration is unavailable.

## Acceptance
- Correction is scope-verified; shots match within scenes.
- The creative look is consistent and preserves skin tones and legal ranges.
- Output conforms to the delivery color space; calibration gaps are noted.
